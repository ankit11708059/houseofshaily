The room

One big floor, and the kit to light it.

It's a single open hall — no fixed seating, nothing bolted down. Clear it for a dance rehearsal, ring it with chairs for a comedy night, or curtain it off and run a workshop.

  • Adjustable track lighting. Multiple spots on ceiling tracks, aimable wherever the action is.
  • Blackout curtains. Run the length of one wall — kill the daylight or split the room.
  • Wide wooden floor. Smooth and continuous — room to move, and to repeat a sequence until it lands.
  • Air conditioning. Comfortable through a long rehearsal or a full house.
  • Chairs, whiteboard, easel. Stackable seating and a board on hand when a session needs one.
