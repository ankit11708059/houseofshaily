House of Shaily

Run your lines

Paste a scene, choose which part you are playing, and it plays back one line at a time. You hear everyone else. Your lines stay covered until you have said them.

Drop a .txt file here, or paste from anywhere — a PDF, an email, a Word document. NAME: line and the name-on-its-own-line format both work. Stage directions in brackets are kept as directions.

Your script never leaves this device. It is parsed in the browser and kept in this browser’s own storage so you can come back to it.
