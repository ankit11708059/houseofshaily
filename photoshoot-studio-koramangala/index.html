Shoot space · Koramangala, Bengaluru

A shoot spacewhere you control the light.

A hall in 1st Block Koramangala for photo shoots, video and content creation. Close the blackout curtains and the only light in the room is the light you bring or turn on; open them and you have grey walls and a wooden floor to work with. Book it by the hour, from 2 hours.

What a shoot gets here

Blackout curtains

Full control of the light, at any hour of the day. A two-hour morning shoot can look like a night scene.

Two looks in one room

Black curtains in for a dark, theatrical background; curtains out for grey walls. Change the look without changing location.

Track lighting

Track-mounted, adjustable lighting is already in the ceiling, so the room is never relying on daylight.

A clear floor

Nothing is fixed in place, so the floor clears for a full-length shot, a dance routine or a set you build yourself.

How the room is set

Shoot layout

Floor cleared, curtains in or out depending on the look. See all four layouts.

In the room

  • Wooden Floor — Large open floor area suitable for movement and workshops.
  • Theatre Lighting — Track-mounted, adjustable lighting suitable for performances and creative sessions.
  • Black Curtains — A theatrical environment that can be adapted to different setups.
  • Flexible Layout — No fixed seating. Configure the space around your activity.
  • Air Conditioning — A comfortable environment for longer sessions.

Questions

Beforeyou book.

Can I shoot here for just two hours?

+

Yes. 2 hours is the minimum, then whole hours up to 12 in one booking — enough for set-up, the shoot and pack-down.

Can the room be made completely dark?

+

Yes. The hall has blackout curtains.

What time can we start?

+

Bookings run from 9am to 11pm, every day of the week.

Where is the space?

+

Chandru Chambers No.21, Basement 1, 7th Main, 1st Cross, 80 Feet Road, 1st Block Koramangala, Bengaluru 560034.

How do I book it?

+

Open the live calendar, pick a date and your hours, and send the request. The time is held in your name while we confirm with you on WhatsApp, the same day. Bookings run in whole hours, 2 hours minimum and up to 12 hours in one booking.

My session needs something that isn't listed here. What do I do?

+

Ask before you book. This page lists what the room has, and nothing it doesn't — we would rather tell you now than on the day.
